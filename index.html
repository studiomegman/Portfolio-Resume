Featured Project · Case Study

3D Printed Vintage Frames.

▶
Watch the reel ↗

Ornate, heirloom-style picture frames designed and 3D printed from scratch, finished with UV-printed photos so they look like antique-shop finds.

389K
Views
12.8K
Saves
14.1K
Shares
The Problem

Real vintage frames are pricey, fragile, and almost never the size you need. I wanted that ornate antique look in a frame anyone could print at home.

The Process

Modeled the ornate frame profiles, printed the frame backings in PETG and ABS, then printed the photos with an xTool UV printer for a finished, gift-ready piece.

The Result

389K views, 12.8K saves, and 14.1K shares on the reel.

Tools Used

[Fusion 360 / Blender], Bambu Studio, PETG & ABS filament, xTool UV printer.

Featured Project

3D Printed PC Library.

A fully 3D-printed miniature library built inside my PC, tiny shelves, books, and details modeled, printed, and assembled to turn a computer case into a little world.

3D Modeling Fabrication Assembly
The Problem

Most PC builds look the same: glass, fans, and RGB. I wanted to turn a computer case into a tiny world a reader would want on their desk.

The Process

Modeled shelves, a staircase, a desk, and miniature books to fit around the hardware, then printed, hand-painted, lit, and assembled every piece.

The Result

500K+ views, 10K+ shares, and 3K+ new followers.

Tools Used

[Fusion 360 / Blender], Bambu Studio, [PLA / PETG] filament, acrylic paint, LED lighting.
